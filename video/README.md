# The Fer Agent video

- `feragent-es.mp4` and `feragent-en.mp4`: the launch video (Spanish and English, 1280×720), shown on the home page.
- The pictures are the real website (this repository), with an example building.
- The voice: OpenAI GPT Audio, through OpenRouter.
- The music is original: `music.py` composes it from scratch in code (pads, a plucked arpeggio, soft bass, kick and hats; D major, 100 BPM), with no samples or third-party audio. `python3 music.py 52 music.wav` makes it again. Like the rest of Fer Agent, it's under the MIT licence.
