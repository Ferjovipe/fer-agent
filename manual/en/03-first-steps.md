# First steps

The first time you go in (without a key), the **First steps** guide comes up, with three steps: **Your key**, **The boss's brain** and **Its first task**.
- In «Your key» you paste the OpenRouter key and press «Save».
- In «The boss's brain» the search already shows the free brains that use tools (the boss needs them): choose one and press «Use this brain», or «Keep the one it has».
- In «Its first task» you write whatever you want (or tap one of the ideas) and «Send it to Boss»: its chat opens with your message.
- «Skip: I'll do it myself» closes the guide for good; «All settings» goes to Settings. Everything in the guide can also be done from Settings and the boss's profile.

If you already have a key from Claude, OpenAI or Gemini, or a model on your computer, you can start with it without OpenRouter: in the guide, «Add it in Settings → Other APIs».

## 1. The OpenRouter key

OpenRouter is what your agents think with: one key and one balance for hundreds of brains.
1. Create an account at openrouter.ai (with your email, Google or GitHub).
2. In «Credits», add some balance (you pay in advance and only what your agents use is spent). If you only use free brains it may work without balance, but free ones get overloaded often.
3. In «Keys», press «Create Key», give it a spending limit and copy the key (it's only shown once).
4. In Fer Agent: Settings (the cog, top right) → paste the key → «Save».

The key is kept only on your computer.

## 2. Taking off the pause

The building starts paused: nothing spends on its own. The switch with the heart, at the top, says «Paused: nothing spends on its own». While paused, agents only work when you talk to them. Take the pause off when you want them to wake up by themselves.

## 3. Talking to the Boss

Press «Talk to Boss» and ask for whatever you want. The Boss shares out the work among the agents and brings you the result.

## 4. Installing built-in agents

«Built-in agents» button: each agent has its card and its «Install» button. When you install one you choose which brain it thinks with (it comes with a suggestion). You can also install the recommended team at once.

## 5. Creating your agents

- «Create agent»: you fill in its profile.
- Or ask the Agent Creator, in your own words, for the agent you need: it proposes the whole profile and you accept it.

## 6. Whole floors: save, load and share

- **Save to a file**: go into a floor (tap its sign) and press «Save to a file». A file is downloaded with its name, its job and its agents (instructions, objective, brain, heartbeat and capabilities). It doesn't have memory, tasks, spending, documents, mailboxes, Telegram or keys.
- **Load a floor from a file**: «Create floor» → «Load a floor from a file» → «Choose file». Before adding it you see who comes in, what each one does and spends; then «Add this floor to my building».
- **Community floors**: in «Create floor», the list of floors other people have shared in the project. Tap one to see it and add it the same way. Someone else wrote their instructions: read them first.
- The agents of a loaded floor come in with «Ask me first», whatever the file says.
- To share one of yours with the project: save it to a file, check it has no personal data and send it (the instructions are in community/floors/README.md, on GitHub).

## 7. The Helper

In «Built-in agents» there's the **Helper**: its documents are this manual. Ask it how to do anything in Fer Agent and it answers step by step. It only works when you ask it, with the brain you choose (a free one is enough).

## Choosing a brain

In each agent's profile → «Brain and spending»:
- Type part of the name in the search box (claude, gpt, gemini, deepseek…) and tap the one you want.
- One-tap filters: **Free**, **Uses tools** (without this the agent can't browse, delegate or use its capabilities) and **Sees images**.
- The list goes from cheapest to most expensive. Prices are in dollars per million tokens (input / output).
- Free brains work, but they get overloaded often: if they fail, the agent retries by itself.
- For an agent that browses or uses the computer, choose a brain that «uses tools».
