# Email

## Connecting mailboxes (Settings → Email)

You can connect as many mailboxes as you like, from any provider (IMAP to read, SMTP to send).
1. «Connect a mailbox»: type the address. The servers fill in by themselves from the address (you can change them).
2. Password: many providers ask for an «app password» instead of the normal one. Gmail: Google account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords. Outlook/Hotmail: Microsoft account → Security → App passwords.
3. «Check»: tests that it can read and send.
The password is kept only on your computer; the website never sees it.

## Giving it to an agent

In its profile → Capabilities → tick «Email» and choose the mailboxes it may use. It reads without marking anything as read; whether it asks you before sending depends on its permissions.

## The Email agent (built-in agents)

It looks at the mailbox every 10 minutes without spending; if new email comes in, its brain summarises it, sorts it and prepares the replies. Each reply comes to you as an email to «Send» or «Correct».

## Without connecting mailboxes

With «Internet as you», an agent can use your Gmail or Outlook on the web as you (with the session you open once in the agents' Chrome): it opens the email already written and clicks «Send».
