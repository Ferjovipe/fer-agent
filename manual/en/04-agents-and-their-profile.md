# The agents and their profile

Tap an agent in the drawing of the building (or in «Strips») to open its profile. At the top are its buttons: «Talk to…», «Wake up now», «See its floor» and «Delete». The profile has four tabs.

## Identity

- **Name** and **look** («Change its look»: ready-made characters, one drawn from your description —paid, one image— or an image of yours).
- **Instructions**: who it is and how it works. With «Let the Creator write it» you say it in your words and the Agent Creator writes it.
- **Personality**: ready-made ones to tick (Kind, Direct…).
- **Objective**: what it pursues. It reads it every time it wakes up.
- When you change who it is, it offers «Start from scratch»: it clears its conversation and short memory so it doesn't get confused with what came before.

## Work

- **When it works**: «Only when asked» (it doesn't spend while it waits) or «Wakes up by itself» (every so many minutes, hours or days, and what it does when it wakes up). It can also be turned off.
- **Reports to**: who it reports to (any agent; circles aren't allowed).
- **Role** and **floor** where it sits.
- **Tasks**: what it has pending. Any agent can give tasks to another and, if it needs the result, it's woken up when the other finishes.
- **On every heartbeat, without a brain (doesn't spend)**: a skill that runs by itself every time it wakes up (for example, the X Watcher or reading the email).

## Brain and spending

- **Thinks with**: OpenRouter, your own key from another company or a model on your computer.
- **Brain**: with search and filters (Free, Uses tools, Sees images).
- **Priority**: 1 is the most important, 5 the least. When spending gets close to the cap, priority 4 and 5 are frozen first.
- **Own daily cap** (in dollars) and what it has spent.

## Capabilities

What the agent can do, its permissions, its documents and its own Telegram. See the chapters on capabilities, permissions, documents and Telegram.

## Roles

Boss, section head, sub-agent, junior (simple, repetitive jobs with a cheap brain), sage, technician, continuous improvement, creator, JEV, controller and memory.

## Coins and agent of the month

Under each reply there's a gold coin (good work) and a red one (it got something wrong, made something up or made you spend too much). The reason becomes a lesson you approve, and the Boss asks you who learns it: that agent, the ones with its role, everyone or the ones you choose. Coins count for «Agent of the month».
