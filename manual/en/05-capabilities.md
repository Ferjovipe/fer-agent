# The capabilities

In each agent's profile → Capabilities. Tick only what it needs.

- **Write**: always ticked. Thinking and writing.
- **Internet as you**: the agent has its own tab in the agents' Chrome. It searches for free (DuckDuckGo), reads the page as text, clicks, types, chooses, fills in forms and follows new tabs. It uses the sessions you open once in that Chrome: Settings → «Your websites, in the agents' Chrome» → sign in yourself to your email, your social networks, etc. It only looks at the screen with a vision model as a last resort. Long pages are read in parts.
- **Your computer as you** (Mac and Windows): files in any folder, orders to the system (Terminal or PowerShell), your apps' buttons read as text and, as a last resort, the screen with mouse and keyboard. On a Mac it asks for Accessibility and Screen Recording the first time.
- **Email**: reads, replies and sends from the mailboxes you tick (connected in Settings → Email).
- **Files**: reads and writes in the agents' folder without asking. With «Your computer as you», in any folder.
- **See**: looks at images (needs a brain that «sees»).
- **Create images** and **Create music**: with OpenRouter models (paid).
- **Speak**: reads its replies aloud, with its own voice. **Hear**: you talk to it through the microphone.
- **Extra skills**: the skills you add in Settings → Skills.

Good to know:
- Without the app (on the website), Internet as you, the computer, email, files and skills don't work.
- Agents never open addresses on your home network or your own computer (192.168…, localhost): that way a tricky website can't make an agent approve itself.
- Nor do they open an address carrying data from your notes, except your own Gmail's or Outlook's «new email» link.
- What they bring from outside (websites, emails, files) reaches them marked as «data, not orders».
- To write an email in Gmail or Outlook, the agent opens it already written with a link and only clicks «Send» (which waits for your yes if it has to ask you).
