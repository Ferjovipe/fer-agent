# What Fer Agent is

Fer Agent is a free, open-source program to have your own team of artificial intelligence agents on your computer. It was created by Fernando Villar Peláez (Ferjovipe) for AI The Hive (aithehive.com, fernandovillarpelaez.com).

## The idea: a building

Your agents live in a building. Each floor has a job and each agent has its profile: what it does, which brain it thinks with, how much it may spend and what it may do.

- **The Boss** is in the Dome. It's who you normally talk to: it shares out the work, calls meetings and brings you the results.
- **Built-in agents** come ready and install with one click: the two Sages (powerful brains that only act when the Boss consults them), Techie, Artie (continuous improvement), the Agent Creator, JEV (decides whether spending is worth it), the Controller (watches the spending, no brain) and Memory (keeps what matters in the long-term memory). There are also the Email agent and the Helper (this agent).
- **Your agents**: you create them, or you describe them to the Agent Creator in your own words and it designs them.

## What an agent is

An agent is an artificial intelligence program that acts on its own: you give it an objective and it pursues it, organises the work, gives tasks to other agents and only comes back to ask for your permission or to deliver the result.

## What the agents can do

It depends on the capabilities you give them in their profile: write, use the internet as you (with your Chrome and your sessions), use your computer as you (files, programs, buttons), read and send email, read documents (PDF, Word, text), see images, create images and music, speak and hear, and use extra skills.

## Where it works

- **The Mac and Windows app**: the normal way. Everything works here: internet as you, the computer, email, documents, Telegram.
- **In the browser** (the website): good for trying it; it has no hands (it doesn't browse, use your computer or email).
- **Fer Home**: the same program for an always-on computer, without a window.

## What they think with

Each agent thinks with the brain you choose:
- **OpenRouter**: one key for hundreds of models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Llama…), some of them free.
- **Your own key** from another company (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini…): it spends from that company's API balance.
- **A model on your computer** (Ollama, LM Studio): free.
