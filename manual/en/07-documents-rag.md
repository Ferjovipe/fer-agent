# Its documents (each agent's RAG)

Each agent can have its own documents and consult them by itself. In its profile → Capabilities → «Its documents».

- **A folder of yours**: «Choose folder» and pick any folder on your computer. It reads everything in it (and in its subfolders) and whatever you add later.
- **Documents added here**: «Add documents» and upload them from the app. They're kept in the app's data (agent-documents), not in your folders.
- **Open**: you see them in the Finder (Mac) or the Explorer (Windows). **Remove**: takes out one you added.

## What it reads

PDF, Word (.docx; on a Mac also .doc and .rtf) and text (txt, md, csv, html…). A scanned PDF (only an image) has no text and can't be read: the profile says so. Up to 40 MB per document.

## How it uses them

Each time it answers or works, it looks in its documents for the pieces that fit and uses them, saying which file each thing comes from. It also has a tool to search more. It searches by words; if you ask in another language and it finds almost nothing, a cheap brain translates the search words and it searches again.

## Privacy

The documents are read and searched on your computer. Only the pieces that fit travel to the agent's brain, like the rest of what you ask it. Each agent has its own.
