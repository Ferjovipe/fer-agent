# Permissions and approvals

## The three modes (in each agent: Capabilities → Permissions)

- **Ask me first**: anything with an effect outside (sending, publishing, paying, buying, deleting, changing things) waits for your yes.
- **With limits**: it only asks for what you tick: paying or buying, sending or publishing, deleting, anything else with an effect outside.
- **Without asking**: it acts as you. Whatever it does happens under your responsibility.

The agent can't change its own permissions. Reading never asks.

## How you approve

When an agent wants to send something, you get a box like a real email: «Boss wants to send this email», with To, Subject and the whole text, taken from what is really written on the page. You see it in its own chat, in the Inbox and on Telegram. Buttons:
- **Send** (or Pay, Delete…): it's done.
- **Correct**: you write what to change; the agent changes it and shows it to you again.
- **Reject**: it isn't done, and the agent knows.

The technical part (the page, the address, the step) is inside «Details».

Nothing that sends, pays or deletes can ask for your permission without the agent saying exactly what goes out.

## The Inbox

«Inbox» button: everything waiting for your yes (actions, agents the Creator proposes, meetings the Boss proposes, coins, lessons, models Artie proposes and whatever an agent says it's missing).

## If you take a while to answer

The tab of an agent waiting for your yes isn't closed (up to 24 hours). If it closed anyway (for example, because you closed the app), when you approve the agent does it again and asks you again. A step on a website that has been waiting for more than 24 hours expires on its own.
