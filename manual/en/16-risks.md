# What an agent is, and what you risk

An agent is an artificial intelligence program that acts on its own. Today's agents make mistakes: they misunderstand orders, make things up and can be fooled by a website or an email with hidden instructions.

With «Internet as you» and «Your computer as you» an agent uses your sessions and your computer as if it were you: it can send, publish, buy, pay, delete or change things in your name.

You decide, agent by agent, what it does without asking you. Whatever it does happens under your responsibility. Give each one only what it needs, set a spending cap and, for money, use accounts and cards with low limits.
