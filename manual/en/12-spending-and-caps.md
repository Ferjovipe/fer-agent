# Spending and caps

## The counter

At the top you always see what has been spent today and the cap. It counts OpenRouter and also your own keys from other companies (at the public price of the same model). Models on your computer are free.

## The caps (Settings)

- The building's **daily cap** and **monthly cap** (in dollars).
- When spending gets close to the cap, agents are frozen by priority: below 80 % everyone works; from 80 %, priority 4 and 5 are frozen; from 90 %, only priority 1 works (the Boss and the controls); at 100 %, everything is frozen, the Boss too, until the next day or until you raise the cap. The 80 % can be changed.
- Each agent's **own daily cap**, in its profile → Brain and spending.

## The general pause

The switch with the heart: paused, nothing spends on its own (agents only work when you talk to them). «Who spends when waking up» shows who wakes up by itself and how much it would cost.

## Your own keys

A Claude, OpenAI, Gemini… key spends from that company's API balance: what you put in or the API credits your plan includes (for example, Claude Max includes $100 or $200 a month since October 2026). It counts towards the cap.

## To spend little

- Cheap or free brains for the daily work; expensive ones only for the hard stuff (the Sages, who only act when the Boss consults them).
- «Only when asked» for agents that don't need to wake up by themselves.
- JEV decides first whether spending is worth it (for example, before sharing out work or filing a conversation in the memory).
